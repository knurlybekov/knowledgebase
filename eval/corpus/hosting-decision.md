# Hosting provider decision (March 2025)

## Problem
The emergency management platform ran on a single on-premises server with no failover. Outages during the 2024 flood season delayed plan updates.

## Decision
In March 2025 the platform team selected provider Alpha for hosting. The selection followed a cost review and a two-week pilot.

## Rationale
Alpha offered Canadian data residency, a signed data processing agreement, and the lowest three-year cost of the options reviewed.

## Participants
The platform lead, the finance analyst and the emergency program manager took part in the review.
